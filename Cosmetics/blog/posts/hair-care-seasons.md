# Adapting Your Hair Care to Each Season

## Spring: Renewal

Spring is about renewal and recovery.

- Switch to lightweight conditioners
- Use clarifying shampoo once weekly
- Deep condition on weekends
- Start protection before summer

## Summer: Protection

Heat and sun damage hair significantly.

- Use heat protectant spray
- Reduce blow-drying
- Apply UV protection
- Hydrate deeply at night
- Trim regularly to prevent split ends

## Fall: Preparation

Prepare for drier winter months.

- Increase protein treatments
- Use moisturizing masks
- Add oils to routine
- Trim damaged ends

## Winter: Moisture

Combat dry air and heating systems.

- Use hydrating shampoo and conditioner
- Apply deep conditioning mask weekly
- Use leave-in conditioner
- Minimize heat styling
- Reduce washing frequency

## Year-Round Tips

- Get regular trims (every 6-8 weeks)
- Protect hair while sleeping
- Stay hydrated
- Maintain a healthy diet
- Use quality products

Your hair will thank you!
