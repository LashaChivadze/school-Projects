# Natural Ingredients in Cosmetics

## Why Choose Natural?

Natural ingredients offer skin-loving benefits without harsh chemicals.

## Popular Natural Ingredients

### Oils
- **Argan Oil**: Hydrating, anti-inflammatory
- **Jojoba Oil**: Mimics skin's natural oils
- **Rosehip Oil**: Rich in vitamins A, C, E

### Extracts
- **Green Tea**: Antioxidant protection
- **Aloe Vera**: Soothing, hydrating
- **Chamomile**: Calming, anti-inflammatory

### Butters
- **Shea Butter**: Deep moisturizing
- **Cacao Butter**: Rich, protective
- **Coconut Oil**: Nourishing, protective

## Benefits of Natural Cosmetics

✓ Gentler on skin
✓ Fewer side effects
✓ Environmentally friendly
✓ Sustainable sourcing
✓ Supporting natural beauty

## Reading Labels

- Look for recognizable ingredients
- Research unfamiliar terms
- Check percentage of natural ingredients
- Watch for greenwashing

Choose products that align with your values while caring for your skin!
